Mentors

Kids learn faster next to real builders.

At Promethia, children don't just have teachers. They have mentors — people who build, design, code, start companies and perform for a living — sitting beside them at the workbench.

What mentors do

Less lecturing. More “show me what you've got.”

Build alongside

Mentors work at the same tables, on the same problems, modelling how professionals actually think.

Ask great questions

Instead of giving answers, mentors ask the question that unlocks the next step.

Give real feedback

Specific, kind and honest — the way builders give feedback to each other.

Open doors

Stories from their careers show kids what's possible — and that every expert was once a beginner.

The mentorship model

Three layers of support around every child.

Every camper is mentored — and, just as importantly, every camper mentors someone else.

01

Camp Guides

Trained, full-time guides lead each pod every day. They know every child by name, run the daily rhythm and keep everyone safe.

02

Builder Mentors

Working professionals from Bangalore's builder community visit for workshops, weekly check-ins, critiques and Demo Day panels.

03

Peer Mentors

Seniors become “Senior Buddies” to Junior pods. Juniors mentor each other in “teach-back” sessions. Teaching is the deepest learning.

Meet the roles

The kinds of people in the room.

Our mentor bench changes each summer. These are illustrative personas showing the kinds of builders campers work with — not specific individuals.

Sample persona

Maker-in-Residence

Hardware tinkerer & workshop lead

Runs the build floor. Has spent years making furniture, drones and contraptions, and believes duct tape is a design material.

Brings to camp: Safe tool skills, rapid prototyping, the joy of version two.

Sample persona

Founder-in-Residence

Startup founder & product builder

Has started companies and learned (mostly the hard way) how to find problems worth solving and pitch to people who say no.

Brings to camp: Problem finding, user interviews, pitching and handling tough questions.

Sample persona

Storyteller-in-Residence

Theatre artist & speaking coach

Trains young speakers the way coaches train athletes: warm-ups, drills, game time. Turns nervous energy into stage presence.

Brings to camp: Voice, story structure, improv and Demo Day coaching.

Sample persona

Engineer-in-Residence

Software engineer & game developer

Builds software by day and silly games by night. Translates code into something an 8-year-old can play with in ten minutes.

Brings to camp: Block and text coding, game design, debugging as detective work.

Sample persona

Designer-in-Residence

Product & visual designer

Draws on everything. Teaches kids that design is about people first, and that a good sketch beats a long explanation.

Brings to camp: Design sprints, paper prototyping, sketching and visual storytelling.

Sample persona

Curiosity Coach

Scientist & field-trip lead

Leads expeditions around the city and runs experiments that occasionally go bang. Asks “how do you know?” a lot.

Brings to camp: Fair tests, observation, measuring, and loving being wrong early.

Peer mentoring

The Senior Buddy program.

Once a week, Build & Pitch campers become coaches. Each Senior is paired with a small group of Curiosity Makers to help them plan, build and practise their show-and-tell.

Juniors get a cool older friend who believes in them. Seniors learn patience, clear explanation and leadership — and discover just how much they already know.

  • Short mentor-training session before the first buddy day
  • Always supervised by a camp guide
  • Seniors reflect on their mentoring in their logbook
  • Buddies cheer each other on at Demo Day

Safety & standards

Every adult is chosen with care.

All camp guides and mentors are screened before working with children, briefed on our child-safety and conduct policy, and never left alone one-on-one with a camper. Visiting mentors always work alongside a camp guide.

In partner schools, Promethia-trained facilitators lead the weekly sessions, follow both our policy and the school's own, and work alongside the school's teachers. How school partnerships work →
