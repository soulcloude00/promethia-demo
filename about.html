About Promethia

The city learns by making.

Promethia exists because children learn the most when their hands are busy, their questions are real, and someone is waiting to see what they made.

Our mission

Raise a generation that builds, speaks up and figures things out.

Most children spend their year being told the answer. At Promethia they spend their summer finding one — and then building it, testing it, and standing up to explain it.

And it doesn't have to wait for summer. Through weekly in-school sessions with CBSE and international schools, students build, speak and solve problems every week of the school year.

We named ourselves after Prometheus, who in the old story carried fire to people so they could make things. Our version is gentler: we hand kids the tools, the time, the mentors and the stage, and get out of the way. The fire is theirs.

In the summer — and every week of the school year.

Our philosophy

Three rules we never break.

Learn by making

Understanding comes through the hands. Kids learn aerodynamics by launching rockets, empathy by interviewing strangers, and persuasion by actually pitching. Theory arrives when it's needed — never before.

Every child ships, every time

Every session ends with something finished enough to show. Not perfect — finished. Shipping small and often builds a habit most adults never learn: starting, finishing, and improving.

No rote. No marks.

There are no tests and no rankings. The Artifact Wall is the report card: a public, growing record of everything a child has made — and the best conversation-starter at the dinner table.

Who it's for

For curious kids — not just the “science” kids.

Promethia is for children in Grades 1 to 10 who like to ask “why?”, take things apart, draw on everything, or talk a lot (we'll teach them to do it on stage).

You don't need any prior experience in coding, robotics or public speaking. Shy kids are especially welcome: our small-steps approach to speaking means the child who whispered on Monday is often the one asking for the microphone by week three.

The tinkerer

Already turning the living room into a workshop. We'll give them better tools and bigger problems.

The storyteller

Full of ideas and opinions. We'll help them build those ideas and present them with clarity.

The quiet observer

Notices everything, says little. We'll give them the confidence to share what they see.

Why Bangalore

A city of builders, right outside the door.

Bangalore is home to rocket scientists, startup founders, product designers, master craftspeople and some of the most inventive street-side repair shops in the world.

We use the city as a classroom. Campers interview vendors at local markets, visit maker spaces and workshops, study how the metro moves people and why traffic doesn't, and invite people from the city's builder community in as mentors and Demo Day guests.

  • Real problems close to home — water, traffic, waste, lonely grandparents, crowded school gates.
  • Real people to learn from — engineers, designers, founders and craftspeople who build for a living.
  • Kannada, English, Hindi, Tamil — all welcome. Kids pitch and interview in whatever language helps them think best.
  • Timed for Karnataka school holidays — April to early June.
  • Inside the city's schools, too — weekly sessions with partner CBSE and international schools through the year.

What makes us different

Not another summer class.

Plenty of camps keep kids busy. We want them to come back changed: more capable, more curious, and braver.

Our promises to parents

Bold with ideas. Careful with kids.

Safe by design

Background-checked adults, supervised tool use, child-safe materials and clear pick-up rules.

Small groups

Low camper-to-guide ratios so no child is invisible. Every child is known by name.

You'll see the work

A weekly update with photos of your child's artifacts (never shared publicly without consent).

Kindness first

We celebrate effort and courage, not just results. Feedback is always specific and kind.
