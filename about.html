About Us

We hand children the spark, not the answers.

Promethia is a Bangalore-based, in-person learning programme for school students. We're starting with a short pilot in partner schools, teaching children to make things, solve real problems and speak up about what they made.

Our storyMeet the team

Our mission

Raise builders who can think, make and speak.

Marks measure what a child can remember. We care about what a child can do: notice a problem, build something that helps, take feedback, try again, and stand up to explain it.

So we run a simple, demanding ritual: a small weekly ask, real materials, real people to build for, and a moment on stage every session. Over time, children build a body of work and the confidence that comes from finishing things.

The Fire of Knowledge

Why we're called Promethia.

In Greek myth, Prometheus was a Titan whose name means “forethought”. He saw people huddled in the cold and dark, so he climbed Olympus, took a spark of fire from the gods and carried it down to humanity, hidden in a hollow fennel stalk.

Fire changed everything. With it, people cooked, forged tools, lit the night and gathered to tell stories. The gift wasn't a finished thing. It was the means to make things.

That's the idea we're named for. Knowledge is fire: it spreads by being shared, it grows when you tend it, and it's only real when you use it to build something. Our job is to put that spark in children's hands, and to teach them to carry it.

The spark that ignites potential. The flame that's yours to keep.

Philosophy

How we teach, in six rules.

Learn by making

Every idea becomes a thing you can hold, test and break. Cardboard first, code when it helps, and always a version two.

Every child ships

Every session ends with every student presenting. No child is left off the stage.

No rote, no screens-first

No worksheets to memorise, no marks to chase. We assess artifacts, videos and growth against each child's own baseline.

Real people, real problems

Kids interview people outside the room (a guard, a shopkeeper, a grandparent) and build for them.

Kind, specific, helpful

Critique is a skill. Thursday is critique day, and everything goes through drafts.

Progress over polish

We reward the biggest leap, the best comeback and the most helpful mentor, never just the shiniest result.

Made in Bangalore

Local by design. That's the point.

We're deliberately rooted in one city. Density beats reach: our facilitators, near-peer mentors and guest builders all live here, and our partner schools are a short drive apart.

  • Mentors from Bangalore's engineers, founders, designers, doctors and artisans
  • Field trips to markets, maker spaces and workshops around the city
  • Projects about real local problems, from the summer heat to the school bus
  • Talks welcome in English, Kannada, Hindi or a mix
