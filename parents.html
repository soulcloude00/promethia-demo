The skills marks can't measure

We assess the artifacts, never tests: journals, prototypes, interview logs, weekly updates and videos, scored on a simple five-part rubric. Juniors see stars and a narrative comment, never a rank. In the 2026 school pilot, parents receive a short strengths-based scorecard every week: what went well, the evidence, and one next step. About the pilot scorecard.

  • Confidence speaking to a room
  • Asking good questions and really listening
  • Finishing what they start
  • Taking feedback without falling apart
  • Working in a team with rotating roles
  • Breaking a big problem into small steps
  • Explaining how something works
  • Helping younger kids learn

Safety & safeguarding

Trust is built in person. We publish our standards.

You'll meet the adults who work with your child, see where they work, and know exactly what our rules are.

  • Child-protection policy

    Written and aligned with POCSO. Every adult briefed before day one.

  • Two-adult rule

    No adult or guest is ever alone with a child. Near-peer mentors never supervise alone.

  • Background verification

    Staff and volunteers are verified per Promethia policy before working with children.

  • Consent-first photos & data

    Separate opt-ins for assessment recordings, sharing with you, and any public use. Verifiable parental consent, as India's DPDP Act requires.

  • Tool licences

    Kids earn a sticker per tool after demonstrating safe use. Colour-coded stations: green, yellow (with mentor), red (adult-run).

  • Age-safe materials

    No coin or button cells for Grades 1–5. Safety glasses at cutting and drilling stations. Soldering only for Gr 8+ at an adult-run station.

  • Pickup lists

    Children are released only to parents or pre-authorised adults.

  • Heat & health plan

    Water every 30 minutes, outdoor work before 11 am, shaded breaks, a nut-free shared snack table and a trained first-aider on site.

Hands first, screens second

Your child already has enough screen time.

Children's doctors in India advise limiting recreational screen time and never letting it replace physical activity or family time. We agree. Promethia is paper-first and hands-on by design.

  • Grades 1–3: no screens, or almost none. Cardboard, cups, tape and copper-tape circuits.
  • Grades 4–5: an optional tablet per squad, for photos and simple block coding.
  • Grades 6–10: tech is a tool, not the point: code, electronics or 3D only when the project needs it.
  • AI, unplugged first: sorting games and paper classifiers before any app.

A note on affordability

Our core kit is deliberately low-cost and paper-first: cardboard from local shops, cups, straws and tape, so cost is never the barrier. In partner schools the programme is typically school-paid or sponsored, which can mean no extra fee for families. Summer camp fees will be published with confirmed dates. Fees TBC
