Outcomes

What your child takes away from the pilot.

Five to seven sessions is short, so we focus on progress you can actually notice.

A stronger voice

A first and a final speaking clip, recorded with consent, so you can hear the change.

Careful AI habits

How to ask AI good questions and check its answers, always supervised.

Real design work

The weekly scorecard

Strengths first. No ranks.

After each week you get a short note: what your child did well, the evidence, and one next step. They're compared only with themselves.

What each session covers
  • Five strengths: spoke up, asked well, understood people, designed it, helped others
  • Levels: Starting, Growing, Strong, Soaring
  • Evidence from the session, such as a clip, a design or a prompt
  • One question to ask at dinner

Safety & safeguarding

Our standards, in writing.

You'll know who works with your child and exactly what our rules are.

  • Child-protection policy

    Written and aligned with POCSO. Every adult briefed before the first session.

  • Two-adult rule

    No adult or guest is ever alone with a child.

  • Background checks

    Facilitators are verified before they work with children.

  • Consent-first photos & data

    Separate opt-ins for recordings, sharing with you and any public use, as India's DPDP Act requires.

  • Supervised AI only

    Students never get their own AI accounts. AI runs through a facilitator-led Class Terminal.

  • Inside school hours

    Sessions run in school, in school slots, with school staff nearby.

Questions parents ask

Straight answers.

Does it cost anything?

The pilot is mostly free. At most, families cover materials. Some cohorts may ask for a small refundable deposit to hold a seat, returned at the end.

How are students chosen?

Students play a short game called Mind Expedition in the school computer lab. Along with teacher input, it helps us form a group of 30–50. It isn't an exam, and it doesn't rank anyone.

My child is shy. Will they have to present?

Every child presents, at their own pace: in pairs, with a prop or from their seat at first. Growth is measured against their own start.

Will you try to sell me something?

No. We send the weekly scorecard and reply to your questions. No sales calls.

Want to know more?

Write to us about your child. A real person replies within two working days.
