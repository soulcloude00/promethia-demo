NEP 2020 & CBSE alignment

Built for the curriculum you already have to deliver.

NEP 2020 asks for experiential, project-based and competency-based learning. CBSE asks for hands-on skill modules and collaborative AI work. We bring a ready way to deliver both.

  • NEP 2020: experiential, inquiry-led, competency-based learning with real assessment evidence.
  • CBSE skill modules: supports Coding, Artificial Intelligence, and Design Thinking & Innovation.
  • CBSE Computational Thinking & AI: unplugged games and worksheets for younger classes; hands-on group projects and reflective journals for older ones.
  • IB / Cambridge: projects mapped to inquiry and project language with your coordinator.

We map each term's projects to the specific modules your school has adopted. We don't overclaim: we won't promise to make anyone an “AI engineer”.

How a partnership starts

Start with one short pilot: 5–7 sessions.

Low commitment, high visibility. See how the 2026 pilot works. You'll see the evidence on your own wall before you decide to scale.

Partner with us Use the enquiry form

Or email hello@promethia.live. We reply within two working days.

  1. Discovery call

    30 minutes with your principal or activity coordinator: grades, timetable slots, rooms, and any lab you already have.

  2. Shortlist in the lab

    Students play Mind Expedition, a short browser game, in your computer lab. From around 300 players we invite a cohort of 30–50.

  3. Run a short pilot

    5–7 weekday sessions of 60–90 minutes in your timetable: Human Intelligence, supervised AI prompting and Design. Mostly free for families; materials at most.

  4. Showcase & review

    Students present at a showcase. Parents get weekly strengths-based scorecards; you get a short summary, and we decide together whether to continue.

  5. Scale

    Then: more cohorts, a weekly programme and a teacher-champion. Our first cohort is running at a Bangalore school in Nov–Dec 2026.

For school leaders

Questions principals ask us.

When in the timetable does it run?

Either inside the timetable (a skill or activity period) or as an after-school club, 1–2 sessions a week of 45–90 minutes. We schedule around your exams and events.

Do our teachers need to run it?

No. Promethia facilitators lead every session. We train one teacher-champion per school to co-run, which helps it become part of school culture. Your teachers are always welcome in the room.

We have an Atal Tinkering Lab. Is this a duplicate?

It's the opposite. Many ATLs are under-used because trained staff are scarce. We put your lab's kits to work in the Robotics and Building tracks, and the speaking and showcase rituals give it visible output.

How do you measure outcomes?

With evidence, not worksheets: artifacts shipped per student per month, baseline and final speaking videos, a five-part rubric, attendance and return rates, and parent showcase attendance, summarised each term for management.

What does it cost?

The pilot is mostly free: at most, families cover materials, and a small refundable deposit can be used to hold seats. Pricing for the weekly programme depends on grades and format, and we share it after a pilot. There is no lab or equipment purchase.

Do you work with IB and Cambridge schools?

Yes. For international schools we map each term's projects to your inquiry and project frameworks with your coordinator.
