What Promethia is

A Build Club. Not a class. Not tuition. A summer where kids make things that didn't exist before.

No worksheets, no marks, no rote. Our report card is a wall — the Artifact Wall — covered in the things your child has actually made. Our one number that matters: artifacts shipped.

  • Hands first. Most of every day is spent making, testing and fixing — not listening.
  • Real audiences. Kids build for real people: a grandparent, a shopkeeper, a younger sibling, a whole room on Demo Day.
  • Human skills, on purpose. Speaking, listening, teamwork and mentoring are built into every single session.

For schools · all year

Summer camps — and every week, in school.

Promethia partners with CBSE schools and international schools to train students on a weekly basis: 1–2 sessions a week through the school year, in addition to our summer camps.

  • Multi-domain, week by week. Students rotate through eight domain tracks — Human Intelligence, AI, Robotics Development, Building & Making, Design, Public Speaking, Problem Solving and Entrepreneurship.
  • Turnkey. We bring the facilitators, materials, kits and session plans. No lab capex for the school.
  • Outcomes you can see. Shipped artifacts, an in-school Artifact Wall, termly showcases and families in the loop.

Six skill pillars

Skills that school marks can't measure.

Every project at Promethia trains a mix of these. By the end of summer, your child won't just know more — they'll be able to do more.

Build & Make

Cardboard, code, circuits, clay. Every week your child takes an idea from sketch to something that actually works — then makes version two.

Speak & Story

Short talks, show-and-tells and a real stage. Kids learn to explain what they made, why it matters, and what went wrong — out loud, with confidence.

Solve Problems

Real problems from real people. Kids ask better questions, break big things into small steps, test their guesses and learn to love being wrong early.

Mentor & Be Mentored

Builders, founders and designers sit beside kids at the workbench. Older campers coach younger ones. Teaching something is how you truly learn it.

Team Up

Roles, deadlines, disagreements and high-fives. Kids practise the hardest real-world skill: making something great with other people.

Create Boldly

Wild ideas welcome. We protect the weird first draft, because that's where original work comes from — and confidence is built one finished project at a time.

How a session works

Wall → Story → Explore → Make → Show.

Every Promethia session follows the same five-beat rhythm, so kids always know what's next — and always end the day with something to show.

  1. Wall

    We start at the Artifact Wall: what did we ship last time, what broke, what's next?

    ~15 min

  2. Story

    A short, true story — an inventor, a failure, a local hero — that sets up today's challenge.

    ~15 min

  3. Explore

    Poke, test, take apart, interview. Kids find the real problem before they build.

    ~30 min

  4. Make

    The longest block. Sketch, prototype, break it, fix it, ask a mentor, try again.

    ~90 min

  5. Show

    Every child presents. Even a 60-second show-and-tell builds a fearless speaker.

    ~30 min

How a week works

One small ask. One weekly ship.

Inspired by the rhythm of the world's best builder communities: a clear challenge on Monday, real progress every day, and a ship on Friday.

Mon

The Ask

A new weekly challenge drops. Teams form, ideas fly, first sketches go up.

Tue

Go find out

Interviews, field trips and experiments. Who is this for, really?

Wed

Build v1

The ugly first version. Fast, rough and gloriously imperfect.

Thu

Test & mentor

Mentor check-ins, peer feedback, playtests — then version two.

Fri

Ship & Show

Projects go on the Wall. Every child presents to the group.

Demo Day

The summer ends on a stage.

On the final day, families, mentors and invited guests from Bangalore's builder community gather for Demo Day. Every child — every single one — stands up and shows what they made.

  • Juniors run a buzzing science-fair-style expo and give short show-and-tells at their tables.
  • Seniors deliver a three-minute pitch on stage and take real questions from a guest panel.
  • Everyone leaves with a portfolio of artifacts and a story they'll tell for years.

Two age tracks

Same spirit. Right-sized challenges.

Grades 1–5 · Ages ~6–10

Curiosity Makers

Big questions, small hands, lots of glue. Juniors learn that they can make anything they can imagine — and explain it proudly.

  • Physical builds: cardboard, simple machines, paper circuits
  • Story-telling, show-and-tell and a first taste of the stage
  • Play-based problem solving and team games
  • Half-day format with plenty of movement and snacks
Explore Curiosity Makers
Grades 6–10 · Ages ~11–16

Build & Pitch

Real problems, real users, real pitches. Seniors run mini-ventures: find a problem, prototype a solution, and pitch it to a panel.

  • Rocketry, electronics, coding and game design
  • User interviews and design sprints out in the city
  • Pitch practice, storytelling and Demo Day on stage
  • Peer-mentoring the Junior track as “Senior Buddies”
