For Students

Speak up. Ask smart. Make it real.

In the pilot you'll practise talking to a room, learn how to get good answers out of AI (and catch it when it's wrong), and design things in Canva or Figma. Then you present it all at the showcase.

Everyone presents

Three things you'll get better at

Speak, prompt, design.

Human Intelligence

Extempore, storytelling and pitching. Spot a problem people have and pitch your fix in a minute.

AI prompting

Write prompts, compare answers and fact-check them. All on the class screen, run by your facilitator.

Design

Posters and slides in Canva (Grades 5–6). Screens and product pages in Figma (Grades 7–8).

Every session

Four steps, about 60–90 minutes.

Same rhythm every time, so you always know what's coming and you always leave with something to show.

  1. Warm up

    One minute of extempore. Everyone speaks.

  2. Challenge

    One real problem, one new skill.

  3. Make

    Prompt, sketch and design with your team.

  4. Show

    Present it. Get one glow and one grow.

The showcase · early December

Your stage.

At the end of the pilot you present your best work to families and teachers, and answer their questions. By then, standing up to speak will feel normal.

  • Getting in starts with Mind Expedition, a short game in your school's computer lab
  • It's a game, not an exam. Have fun with it
  • Not in the first group? There will be more cohorts

Want in?

Tell your teacher you're interested, and keep an eye out for Mind Expedition in your computer lab.
