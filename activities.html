Activities

Rockets before lunch. Pitches after.

Twenty kinds of activities, one idea: kids learn by doing real things. They power both our summer camps and the weekly sessions we run inside partner schools — each one tied to a domain track, from Human Intelligence and AI to Robotics, Design and Entrepreneurship.

Activity mix varies by track and week. Seniors-only activities are adapted into simpler versions for Juniors where it makes sense. In partner schools, activities are sized to fit a weekly 60–90 minute session (sample format).
