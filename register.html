FAQ

Questions parents ask us.

Can't find your answer? Email hello@promethia.live — a real person will reply.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the track (half-day Curiosity Makers or full-day Build & Pitch) and the cohort. Please contact us for details — we'll send the current fee sheet along with what's included (all materials, tools, the logbook and Demo Day). Sibling and multi-cohort options may be available; just ask.

What are the dates?

We're planning two four-week cohorts for Summer 2027: Cohort A, 12 April – 7 May and Cohort B, 10 May – 4 June (Monday to Friday). These are sample dates and will be confirmed soon — see Dates & schedule.

Where is the camp held?

In Bangalore, at a studio venue with indoor workshop space and an outdoor area for launches and team games. The exact venue will be announced along with confirmed dates. Field expeditions to places around the city are planned and supervised, with parents informed in advance.

What ages is Promethia for?

Children in Grades 1–10 (roughly ages 6–16). Curiosity Makers is for Grades 1–5 and Build & Pitch is for Grades 6–10. If your child is on the border between tracks, tell us a little about them and we'll help you choose.

Does my child need experience in coding, robotics or speaking?

Not at all. Promethia is designed for beginners and enthusiasts alike. Every project has a simple starting point and plenty of room to go further. Shy kids are very welcome — we build up to speaking in small, safe steps.

What should my child bring?

A water bottle, a healthy snack, comfortable clothes that can get a little messy, and closed-toe shoes. Build & Pitch campers should also bring a packed lunch. We provide all tools, materials and the builder's logbook. Please leave expensive gadgets at home.

How do you keep children safe?

Safety comes first. All adults are screened and briefed on our child-safety and conduct policy. Group sizes are small, tools are introduced with training and used under supervision, materials are child-appropriate, and no adult is left alone one-on-one with a child. Children are only released to parents or pre-authorised adults, and we keep emergency contacts and any medical or allergy information on file.

Will my child really present on stage?

Yes — at their own pace. Every session ends with a short show-and-tell, so by Demo Day presenting feels normal. Juniors present at expo tables; Seniors pitch on stage. Nobody is forced, and every attempt is celebrated.

Can we attend Demo Day?

Absolutely — families are the most important audience. Demo Day takes place on the last Friday of each cohort. Details and timings are shared during camp.

Do you run programs in schools?

Yes. Alongside summer camps, we partner with CBSE schools and international schools to run weekly in-school sessions (1–2 per week) across domain tracks including Human Intelligence, AI and Robotics. School leaders can read more on For Schools or email hello@promethia.live. Parents are welcome to share the page with their school.

What's your refund and cancellation policy?

We'll share the full policy along with fees. If you have a specific situation, write to us at hello@promethia.live and we'll do our best to help.
