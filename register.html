FAQ

Questions families ask us.

Can't find your answer? Email hello@promethia.live.

Who is Promethia for?

School students in Bangalore. The 2026 pilot is for Grades 5–8, with separate versions for Grades 5–6 and 7–8.

When does it run?

The first pilot runs in November 2026 on school weekdays, with a showcase in early December. Future cohorts will be announced after that.

How much does it cost?

The pilot is mostly free; at most, families cover materials. Some cohorts may use a small refundable deposit to hold a seat.

Where does it happen?

In person in Bangalore: inside partner schools in Bangalore, during school hours.

My child is shy. Will they have to present?

Every child presents, at their own pace. Shy speakers can present in pairs or squads, with a prop, or from a chair. Growth is measured against their own Day-1 video, not against other kids.

How much screen time is involved?

Very little. Grades 1–3 use none or almost none, Grades 4–5 use an optional tablet per squad, and seniors use tech only when a project needs it.

What should my child bring?

A water bottle, a cap, a snack and closed-toe shoes. Seniors bring a packed lunch on longer days. We provide every tool, material and journal.

How do you keep children safe?

A POCSO-aligned child-protection policy, background-verified staff, a two-adult rule, tool licences, age-safe materials, pickup lists and consent-first photos. See our safety standards.

Will you call me to sell something?

No. We reply to your enquiry by email or WhatsApp, and we'll only call if you ask us to. No upsells, ever.

Do you work with schools?

Yes. We partner with CBSE and international schools starting with a short 5–7 session pilot. See For Institutes or email hello@promethia.live.
