How the partnership works

A build club inside your timetable.

The same Promethia that runs our summer camps — the Artifact Wall, the five-beat session rhythm, every child presenting — brought into your school every week.

CBSE & international schools

We partner with CBSE schools and international schools across Bangalore to bring Promethia into the regular timetable, for Grades 1–10.

Weekly, all year

1–2 sessions per week through the academic year, in your school, during school hours or as an after-school club — scheduled around your timetable and exams.

Turnkey — we bring everything

Materials, tools, kits, robotics hardware, logbooks and the session plans all come with us. No lab to build, no equipment to buy: zero lab capex for the school.

Trained facilitators & mentors

Every session is led by Promethia-trained facilitators, with visiting mentors from Bangalore's builder community. Your teachers are welcome in the room — and to co-facilitate.

Outcomes you can see

No worksheets. Progress is measured in shipped artifacts: an in-school Artifact Wall, a builder's logbook per student, termly showcases and an end-of-year portfolio.

Parents in the loop

Regular photo updates of artifacts (shared through the school, with consent), take-home challenges, and showcase days where families see the work first-hand.

Weekly multi-domain training

Eight domain tracks, rotating week by week.

Each week focuses on one domain track, so over a term students build a broad base — and Human Intelligence (communication, critical thinking, collaboration and leadership) runs through every single session. Every track has a Grades 1–5 and a Grades 6–10 version.

A weekly session

Wall → Story → Explore → Make → Show, in one period.

Our five-beat rhythm, compressed to fit a school block. Students always know what's next — and always leave with something to show.

Sample timings for a 75-minute session

Wall · ~5 min

Last week's ships

What did we make, what broke, what's next?

Story · ~10 min

A true story

An inventor, a failure, a local hero — framing today's challenge.

Explore · ~10 min

Find the problem

Poke, test, take apart, ask.

Make · ~35 min

Build it

Sketch, prototype, test, fix — with facilitators at the table.

Show · ~15 min

Every student presents

One “glow” and one “grow” from peers.

With two sessions a week, the second session goes deeper into the week's track — or adds a dedicated Human Intelligence / public-speaking module.

Parent engagement

Families see the work, not just the timetable.

Parents are the most important audience. We keep them close — through the school, and always with consent.

  • Artifact updates — photos of what students made, shared via the school (we photograph artifacts, not children, unless parents consent).
  • Take-home challenges — small weekly builds and conversation starters for the dinner table.
  • Showcase days — families watch every student present.
  • Parent sessions — short open sessions on how kids learn by making, and on raising thoughtful AI users.

FAQ for schools

Questions principals ask us.

Something else? Email hello@promethia.live — a real person will reply.

Which schools do you work with?

We partner with CBSE schools and international schools (including those following international curricula) for Grades 1–10. Every partnership is set up individually with the school's leadership.

How often do sessions run?

1–2 sessions per week, per class or group, through the academic year. A typical session fits a 60–90 minute block such as a double period (sample format) — we adapt to your timetable, exam calendar and events.

What does the school need to provide?

A room (a regular classroom or activity room works), a timetable slot, and a point of contact. We bring all materials, tools, kits and robotics hardware — there is no lab to set up and no equipment to buy.

How does it fit our curriculum?

Promethia complements the experiential, competency-based and skills-focused learning that CBSE and international curricula encourage. We don't replace subject teaching; we give students a weekly place to apply it. We're happy to walk your academic team through how sessions connect to your school's goals.

How are AI sessions kept age-appropriate?

Younger grades learn AI ideas mostly unplugged — through games, sorting and role-play. Older grades use beginner-friendly, supervised tools, with a strong focus on checking AI answers and using AI responsibly. No student accounts are required without the school's approval.

How do we measure outcomes?

Through what students actually make and show: an in-school Artifact Wall, a builder's logbook per student, facilitator observation notes across the domain tracks, termly showcases and an end-of-year portfolio summary shared with the school and families.

What about safety and conduct?

All Promethia facilitators are screened and briefed on our child-safety and conduct policy, and follow your school's own policies on campus. Tools are introduced with training and used under supervision, with child-appropriate materials.

What does it cost?

Partnership fees depend on the number of grades, sections and sessions per week. Write to us and we'll send a proposal for your school.
