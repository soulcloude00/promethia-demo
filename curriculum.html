Curriculum · Pilot 2026

Seven sessions, one step at a time

What students make and practise, session by session.

About the pilot All programs

In short

  • 5–7 sessionsNovember weekdays
  • 60–90 minPer session
  • Two tracksGr 5–6 and Gr 7–8
  • ShowcaseEarly December

Working outline. Final activities are set with each host school.

1Speak upWhat do I want people to hear?

Grades 5–6

Make
Canva poster: my name, an interest, a school problem.
Practise
30-second extempore

Grades 7–8

Make
Figma intro card: who I am, what I care about.
Practise
60-second extempore (baseline clip)

Note: Baseline clip (with consent). First scorecard goes home.

2Ask wellHow do I get a useful answer from AI?

Grades 5–6

Make
Write, test and improve three prompts.
Practise
Say which worked best, and why

Grades 7–8

Make
Prompt a summary, then fact-check it.
Practise
Two-minute talk: “what the AI got wrong”

Note: All AI runs on the supervised Class Terminal. No student accounts.

3Spot a needWho has a problem I can help with?

Grades 5–6

Make
Interview a classmate; write a “How might we…?”
Practise
Tell their story in one minute

Grades 7–8

Make
Interview three people; pick one problem.
Practise
Problem pitch: who, what, why

Note: Light entrepreneurship: needs before ideas.

4Design itWhat would the fix look like?

Grades 5–6

Make
Canva poster or flyer for the idea.
Practise
Peer feedback: one glow, one grow

Grades 7–8

Make
Figma screen or product page.
Practise
Defend one design choice

Note: AI for ideas; design decisions are theirs.

5Pitch itHow do I get people to say yes?

Grades 5–6

Make
Three-slide pitch: problem, idea, ask.
Practise
90-second pitch with a partner

Grades 7–8

Make
Pitch deck, five slides max.
Practise
Two-minute pitch with live Q&A

Note: Rehearsal with feedback.

6–7ShowcaseWhat can I do now that I couldn't before?

Grades 5–6

Make
Final poster and pitch.
Practise
Present at the showcase

Grades 7–8

Make
Final design and deck.
Practise
Pitch and take questions

Note: Early December, for families and teachers. Final scorecard vs. their own start. Sessions 6–7: one or two slots.

The scorecard

Five strengths. Scored on evidence.

Starting · Growing · Strong · Soaring. Private, weekly, no ranks.

  • Spoke up

    Clarity & confidence

  • Asked well

    Prompting & checking AI

  • Understood people

    Empathy & problem finding

  • Designed it

    Visual design

  • Helped others

    Teamwork

Kept in every session

Everyone speaks. Every team shows. AI stays supervised.
