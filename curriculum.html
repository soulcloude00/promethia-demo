Curriculum · Draft v1

Six weeks, week by week.

The summer camp outline for both tracks: what kids build, ship and practise each week, and how each Friday showcase works. School-program sprints follow the same arc across 12 weeks.

Back to ProgramsGet Started

Working draft, subject to change. This outline is Promethia's own design, adapting widely used formats (design thinking, story spines, weekly updates, demo days). Activities, timings and showcase formats will be refined during the pilot. Draft

01SparkWhat can my hands make?

Makers · Gr 1–5

Build
Three guided builds: a cardboard marble run, a cup-and-straw wobble-bot, a copper-tape light-up card (AA packs only). Thursday: remix one.
Ships
Remixed build + an “I made / I learned / next I will” card
Speaking
Name + one thing → 30-second show-and-tell, holding the object

Builders · Gr 6–10

Build
Skills sampler: a cardboard mechanism, a simple circuit, a make-it-in-60-minutes digital build. Start a Problem Hunting Journal.
Ships
Remixed build + Weekly Update #1 + a one-line idea draft
Speaking
Self-introduction, 30–45-second show-and-tell, first daily table topics

Friday & mentors: Baseline speaking video on Monday. Friday: Show-and-Tell Circle (juniors) and Lightning Round (seniors). Parents invited.

02ToyWho has a problem I can help?

Makers · Gr 1–5

Build
“Make a toy for someone younger.” Interview a sibling, cousin or buddy with three questions, then build toy v1.
Ships
Toy v1 + a drawing of the person it's for
Speaking
Story spine, told about the person they interviewed

Builders · Gr 6–10

Build
Pick the capstone problem from the journal. Interview at least 3 real people about past behaviour. Build the roughest toy version.
Ships
Toy v1 + Weekly Update #2 + “I'm making ___ for ___ because ___.”
Speaking
60-second story spine about the interviewee's problem

Friday & mentors: Toy Table Playtest (juniors) and Toy Demo Rooms (seniors). Near-peer mentor training for selected Gr 9–10s.

03CareWill anyone care?

Makers · Gr 1–5

Build
Choose a capstone from a menu: a toy, a helper for a guard or teacher, “Beat the Bangalore heat”, or an organiser for family.
Ships
Capstone v1 + a “Who it's for” poster
Speaking
60-second “I made / because / next” talk with a 3-box card

Builders · Gr 6–10

Build
Test toy v1 with at least 5 real users, add an expert interview, build v2 and define a success measure.
Ships
v2 + Weekly Update #3 + a decision log
Speaking
Organised 1–2-minute talk: hook → problem → idea → learning

Friday & mentors: Mid-camp Open Studio: parents become testers, not builders. Near-peer mentors join junior squads.

04IterateHow can it be better?

Makers · Gr 1–5

Build
Capstone v2 through “change one thing” challenges: stronger, clearer, more fun. Photograph v1 next to v2.
Ships
Capstone v2 + a Before/After card
Speaking
Gestures, a big voice and eye contact

Builders · Gr 6–10

Build
v3 through deliberate experiments: change one variable, test, record. Keep a feedback log and note trade-offs.
Ships
v3 + Weekly Update #4
Speaking
Voice, pace, pauses + “hot seat” Q&A drill

Friday & mentors: Before & After Gallery (juniors) and Hot-Seat Showcase (seniors). Guest professionals run a Feedback Clinic.

05Tell & GrowHow do I get people to say yes?

Makers · Gr 1–5

Build
Polish and package: a name, logo, poster and “how to use” card, plus small extras for Mini-Market.
Ships
Market-ready poster + display + progress recording
Speaking
60–90-second demo with a prop: What is it? → Watch it work → What's next?

Builders · Gr 6–10

Build
Brand it, price it, and draft a 90-second demo video and a pitch deck of six slides or fewer.
Ships
Demo video draft, deck draft, Weekly Update #5, progress recording
Speaking
90-second demo in four beats + a 2–3-minute pitch draft; mock panel

Friday & mentors: Mini-Market with camp coins (optional real small-value sales donated to a cause). Seniors run stalls and a pitch run-through.

06DemoWhat can I build now that I couldn't six weeks ago?

Makers · Gr 1–5

Build
Final fixes, booth setup and a portfolio folder: journal, six ship photos, Before/After card.
Ships
Demo Day booth + portfolio
Speaking
Final recording (same prompt as Day 1) and daily squad rehearsal

Builders · Gr 6–10

Build
Final version + a project page: interviews, decision log, weekly updates, demo video, deck.
Ships
Final demo video + live pitch
Speaking
Final recording + full pitch rehearsals with Q&A

Friday & mentors: DEMO DAY: gallery, 90-second demos, senior pitch panels, awards for growth, and every child takes their portfolio home.

Assessment

Five criteria. Scored on evidence, not impressions.

Levels: Starting · Growing · Strong · Soaring. “Strong” is the target for most kids by Week 6. Scored at the midpoint (shared privately with parents) and at the end.

  • Built it

    Making & creativity

  • Improved it

    Iteration

  • Understood the user

    Empathy & problem solving

  • Told the story

    Speaking

  • Helped others

    Teamwork & mentoring

Compressing to four weeks

Weeks 2+3 and 4+5 merge. Juniors choose a capstone on Monday of Week 2; seniors pick, interview, build and test within the week. Kept no matter what: every Friday ship, the baseline and final videos, Thursday critique, and at least one parent showcase before Demo Day.

Daily rhythm

Mon Spark · Tue Explore · Wed Build · Thu Feedback · Fri Ship. Juniors work in 20–40-minute blocks with movement between; seniors in 60–90-minute build blocks with a daily speaking lab.
