Curriculum · Pilot 2026

Seven sessions, one at a time.

What pilot students make and practise in each session, for Grades 5–6 and Grades 7–8. Sessions run on school weekdays in November, about 60–90 minutes each.

About the pilot All programs

Pilot outline. Five to seven sessions depending on each school's timetable. Exact activities are set with the host school and adjusted to the group.

1Speak upWhat do I want people to hear?

Grades 5–6

Make
Name, one interest, one problem I notice at school. Poster sketch in Canva.
Practise
30-second extempore on a picked topic

Grades 7–8

Make
Personal intro card in Figma: who I am, what I care about.
Practise
60-second extempore, first recorded baseline

Note: Baseline speaking clip recorded (with consent). First parent scorecard goes home.

2Ask wellHow do I get a useful answer from AI?

Grades 5–6

Make
Write, test and improve three prompts on the facilitator-run Class Terminal.
Practise
Explain which prompt worked best, and why

Grades 7–8

Make
Prompt a research summary, then fact-check it against a second source.
Practise
Two-minute talk: “what the AI got wrong”

Note: No student accounts: all AI use runs through the supervised Class Terminal.

3Spot a needWho has a problem I can help with?

Grades 5–6

Make
Interview a classmate or teacher; write one “How might we…?”
Practise
Tell their story in one minute

Grades 7–8

Make
Interview three people; pick one problem and size it.
Practise
Problem pitch: who, what, why it matters

Note: Light entrepreneurship starts here: needs before ideas.

4Design itWhat would the fix look like?

Grades 5–6

Make
Design a poster or flyer for the idea in Canva.
Practise
Peer feedback: one glow, one grow

Grades 7–8

Make
Design a simple screen or product page in Figma.
Practise
Defend one design choice under questions

Note: Students use AI prompts for ideas, then make their own design decisions.

5Pitch itHow do I get people to say yes?

Grades 5–6

Make
Three-slide pitch: problem, idea, ask.
Practise
90-second pitch with a partner

Grades 7–8

Make
Short pitch deck (five slides or fewer).
Practise
Two-minute pitch with live Q&A

Note: Rehearsal with feedback from facilitators and teachers.

6–7ShowcaseWhat can I do now that I couldn't before?

Grades 5–6

Make
Final poster and pitch.
Practise
Present at the showcase

Grades 7–8

Make
Final design and deck.
Practise
Pitch and take questions at the showcase

Note: Early December showcase for families and teachers. Final scorecard compares each child with their own start, never with others. Sessions 6 and 7 run as one or two slots, depending on the school timetable.

The scorecard

Five strengths. Scored on evidence.

Levels: Starting · Growing · Strong · Soaring. Shared privately with parents every week. Each child is compared only with their own start. No ranks.

  • Spoke up

    Clarity & confidence

  • Asked well

    Prompting & checking AI

  • Understood people

    Empathy & problem finding

  • Designed it

    Visual design

  • Helped others

    Teamwork

Kept in every session

Every student speaks. Every team shows something. AI only runs through the supervised Class Terminal.

After the pilot

If the pilot works, the weekly school programme in 2027 builds on these sessions and adds more domains over the year. What comes after.
