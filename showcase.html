Demo Day

How Demo Day works.

A half-day celebration on the final Friday of each cohort. Families, mentors and guests from Bangalore's builder community are invited.

  • The Expo. Curiosity Makers run tables with their builds and give 60-second show-and-tells to visitors.
  • The Stage. Build & Pitch teams deliver 3-minute pitches, then take questions from a guest panel.
  • The Wall. Every artifact from the summer is displayed — v1s included, because the journey is the point.
  • In partner schools. Students in our weekly school program get their own in-school Artifact Wall and termly showcases. For Schools →
  • The Send-off. Every child receives their portfolio and logbook to take home.

Photos & privacy

Your child's work, shared your way.

We photograph artifacts — not children — for our weekly family updates. Photos of campers are only ever taken and shared with written parental consent, and you can opt out at any time. Artifacts shown on this website are illustrative samples.
