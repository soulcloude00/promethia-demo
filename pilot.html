Domain 01

Human Intelligence

Public speaking, extempore and light entrepreneurship.

What students do

  • Extempore and storytelling
  • Interview people, find a real need
  • Pitch an idea (light entrepreneurship)
SessionsSessions 1, 3 and 5: Speak up, Spot a need, Pitch it
Grades 5–630-second extempore, one-minute story, 90-second pitch
Grades 7–860-second extempore, problem pitch, two-minute pitch with live Q&A
At the showcasePresent to families and teachers, and take questions

Domain 02

AI prompting

Ask AI good questions, then check the answers. Supervised; no student accounts.

What students do

  • Write, test and improve prompts
  • Compare answers and fact-check them
  • Use AI for ideas, then decide for themselves
SessionsSession 2 (Ask well), then used in Session 4
Grades 5–6Three prompts, then explain which worked best
Grades 7–8Prompt a summary, fact-check it, two-minute talk on what the AI got wrong
At the showcaseIdeas found with AI, shaped into their own pitch. Prompts they wrote count as scorecard evidence

Domain 03

Design

Design for real people, in Canva or Figma.

What students do

  • Design for a real person's need
  • Peer feedback: one glow, one grow
  • Defend a design choice
SessionsSessions 1, 4 and 6–7: intro, Design it, Showcase
Grades 5–6Canva: posters, flyers and pitch slides
Grades 7–8Figma: intro card, a screen or product page, pitch deck
At the showcaseFinal poster (Gr 5–6) or final design and deck (Gr 7–8)

Shortlisting

Mind Expedition: a game, not an exam

A short browser game in one lab period, first week of November. It shows how students tackle puzzles and explain their thinking.

One input, alongside teachers. Not an IQ test, no ranks shared, and everyone is welcome in later cohorts.
