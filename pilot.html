Pilot · Nov–Dec 2026

A short, proof-of-work pilot with partner schools in Bangalore.

Our first cohort runs at a Bangalore school this November: 30–50 Grade 5–8 students, 5–7 in-school sessions, and a showcase in early December. Small on purpose, so the evidence is real before we grow.

See the pilot timeline Host a cohort at your school

At a glance

What the pilot is, and what it isn't.

It is a short, supervised programme designed to show real work in a few weeks. It is not a year-long course, a coaching class or a talent ranking.

30–50 students

Grades 5–8, selected from a wider group through a short lab-based game.

5–7 sessions

Weekday school slots of about 60–90 minutes through November.

Early-December showcase

Students present what they built and said to families and teachers.

Mostly free

Families cover materials at most. A refundable deposit may be used to hold a seat.

Three domains

Speak, prompt, design.

The pilot focuses on three domains from our wider curriculum, chosen because students can show visible progress in 5–7 sessions.

Human Intelligence

Public speaking and extempore practice every session, plus light entrepreneurship: spotting a need, shaping an idea, pitching it in a minute.

AI prompting

Learning to ask AI good questions and check its answers, in a supervised Class Terminal run by the facilitator. No student accounts, no unsupervised AI use.

Design

Visual design for real audiences: Canva for Grades 5–6, Figma for Grades 7–8. Posters, pitch slides and simple screens.

Shortlisting

Mind Expedition: a short game, not an exam.

Students play Mind Expedition, a browser game, during a lab period in the first week of November. It gives our facilitators a first look at how students approach puzzles, choices and explaining their thinking.

It is one input for forming a manageable first cohort, alongside teacher input. It is not an IQ test or a measure of a child's ability, and scores are not shared as ranks. Students who aren't in this cohort are welcome in later ones.

Preview Mind Expedition
  • Played in school computer labs, supervised by staff
  • Around 300 students play; 30–50 are invited
  • No student login or personal account needed to play
  • Results reviewed by facilitators, not used to rank children

Timeline

November to early December 2026.

Dates are set with each host school around exams and events.

  1. Early Nov: Shortlisting

    Students play Mind Expedition in the school computer lab. From around 300 players, 30–50 are invited into the cohort.

  2. Nov · weekdays: Sessions 1–7

    5–7 sessions in school slots, across the three domains. Saturdays only if the school chooses.

  3. Weekly: Parent scorecard

    A short strengths-based update after each week. No ranks, no comparisons.

  4. Early Dec: Showcase

    Each student presents work from the pilot. Schools receive a short summary of what happened.

For parents

A weekly scorecard built on strengths.

Every week, parents get a short note on what their child worked on, what they did well and one thing to try next.

  • Strengths first: what your child did well this week
  • Evidence: a speaking clip, a design or a prompt they wrote
  • One clear next step, never a rank or class comparison
  • Consent-first photos and data; nothing published without permission

Host a cohort at your school.

We're working with partner schools in Bangalore and adding a small number of hosts for early 2027. Start with one cohort, one lab period and a showcase.
