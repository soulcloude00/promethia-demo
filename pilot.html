Pilot 2026 · Nov–Dec · partner schools in Bangalore

A short pilot with real proof

30–50 Grade 5–8 students. 5–7 sessions in school. One showcase in early December. Small on purpose, so the results are real before we grow.

Three domains

Speak. Prompt. Design.

Chosen because students can show visible progress in a few weeks.

  • Storytelling

AI prompting

  • Prompt craft

Design

Visual design for real audiences: Canva for Grades 5–6, Figma for Grades 7–8.

  • Canva · Gr 5–6

  • Feedback

Shortlisting

Mind Expedition: a game, not an exam

Students play a short browser game during a lab period in the first week of November. It shows our facilitators how students approach puzzles, choices and explaining their thinking.

It's one input, alongside teacher input, for forming a manageable first cohort. It is not an IQ test, scores aren't shared as ranks, and students who aren't in this cohort are welcome in later ones.

For parents

A scorecard built on strengths

After each week, a short note: what your child did well, the evidence, and one thing to try next.

  • Strengths first, every time
  • Evidence: a clip, a design or a prompt they wrote
  • One clear next step
  • Never a rank or class comparison
  • Consent-first photos and data

FAQ

Pilot questions

Which school is hosting?

Our first cohort runs at a partner school in Bangalore. We name partner schools only with their permission, once results are in.

Is Mind Expedition a test?

No. It's a short game and one input, with teacher input, for forming the cohort. Nobody is ranked.

What happens after the pilot?

If it works, a weekly school programme in 2027, then holiday cohorts for more families. See Programs.

How do I host a cohort at my school?

Email the team. We'll set up a short call about grades, lab access and timetable slots.

Host a cohort at your school

We're adding a small number of partner schools in Bangalore for 2027. Start with one cohort, one lab period and a showcase.
